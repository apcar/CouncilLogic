# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve Model Council. This page is general information,
not legal advice.

## Welcome now

Use the repository issue forms for:

- reproducible bug reports that do not contain secrets or sensitive data; and
- design proposals, research references, threat-model observations, and
  questions about documented behavior.

By submitting issue text, you agree that GitHub and repository maintainers may
display and discuss it under GitHub's terms. Keep reports concise and do not
attach code, documentation patches, generated assets, or other substantial
copyrightable material. Security vulnerabilities belong in
[private vulnerability reporting](docs/SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

## Contributions remain closed

Do not open pull requests or submit code, documentation, tests, generated
assets, or other copyrightable contributions. They will be closed without
review.

The project will not accept external copyrightable contributions until a
counsel-reviewed contributor license agreement grants the rights needed to
maintain the AGPL public core and, if later chosen, offer separately negotiated
commercial licenses. Opening issue discussion does not waive this gate.

Repository-owner maintenance branches and pull requests are not external
contributions, but they remain subject to project review and CI.

All participation must follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
